personal-agent

A private tool by Sriramkishore Naraharisetti

personal-agent is a small program that runs on its developer's own Mac. Each weekday morning it reads his own Gmail and Outlook.com inboxes, read-only, and emails him a single summary: messages that need attention, tasks and deadlines, and a few public market and technology updates.

It is not offered to anyone else. The only Google account it connects to is the developer's own.

  • Gmail access is read-only (gmail.readonly). The tool can't send, delete, move or change mail.
  • It sends its summary only to the developer's own address.
  • Email is summarized with Anthropic's Claude API and isn't shared with anyone else.

Details are in the privacy policy.
